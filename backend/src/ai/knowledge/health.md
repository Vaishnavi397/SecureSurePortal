## HLT-1 Health Cover - Hospitalisation
Reasonable and necessary medical expenses are covered when the insured person is admitted to a network or registered hospital for at least 24 hours, including room rent, surgeon fees, medicines, diagnostics and ICU charges.

## HLT-2 Health Claim Limit
Maximum payable per hospitalisation claim: ₹5,00,000 per policy year. Room rent is capped at 1 percent of the sum insured per day.

## HLT-3 Health Required Documents
Required documents: HOSPITAL_BILL, DISCHARGE_SUMMARY, MEDICAL_REPORT, ID_PROOF, POLICY_COPY. Original itemised hospital bills and the discharge summary are mandatory for reimbursement claims.

## HLT-4 Health Exclusions
The following are excluded from health cover: cosmetic or plastic surgery without medical necessity, pre-existing conditions during the first 24 months of the policy, routine dental treatment unless caused by an accident, self-inflicted injury, and treatment for alcohol or drug abuse.

## HLT-5 Health Waiting Period
A 30-day initial waiting period applies from the policy start date. Hospitalisation during this period is not covered, except for injuries caused by an accident.

## HLT-6 Health Submission Time
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of hospital discharge.

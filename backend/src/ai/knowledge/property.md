## PRP-1 Property Cover - Perils
Buildings and household contents are covered against loss or damage caused by fire, lightning, explosion, flood, storm and burglary.

## PRP-2 Property Claim Limit
Maximum payable per property claim: ₹10,00,000 per policy year. Property claims above ₹5,00,000 require a surveyor report before settlement.

## PRP-3 Property Required Documents
Required documents: POLICY_COPY, DAMAGE_PHOTOS, REPAIR_ESTIMATE, SURVEYOR_REPORT. A police FIR copy is also needed for burglary claims.

## PRP-4 Property Exclusions
The following are excluded from property cover: wear and tear, gradual deterioration, war, deliberate or intentional damage, and loss while the property has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.

## PRP-5 Property Notification
Fire and burglary losses must be notified to the insurer within 48 hours of discovery.

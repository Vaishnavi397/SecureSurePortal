## GEN-1 Claim Processing Lifecycle
Claims move through the stages NEW, UNDER_REVIEW, ADDITIONAL_INFO_REQUIRED, APPROVED or REJECTED, SETTLEMENT_IN_PROGRESS and CLOSED. Only an authorised claims officer may approve or reject a claim.

## GEN-2 Missing Documents Rule
When any required document for the claim type is missing, the claim must be placed in ADDITIONAL_INFO_REQUIRED and the customer must be asked to supply the missing documents before a decision is made.

## GEN-3 Enhanced Verification
Claims with a claimed amount above ₹3,00,000, or incidents within 30 days of the policy start date, require enhanced verification by a senior officer.

## GEN-4 AI Assessment Policy
AI generated assessments are advisory only. The claims officer remains responsible for the final decision and the AI must not approve or reject claims.

## GEN-5 Settlement Timeline
Approved claims must be settled within 15 working days of approval. The claim is CLOSED once settlement is complete.

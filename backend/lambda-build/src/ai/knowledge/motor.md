## MOT-1 Motor Cover - Accidental Damage
Own-damage to the insured vehicle caused by a road accident, collision, overturning, fire, flood or theft is covered up to the insured declared value of the vehicle.

## MOT-2 Motor Claim Limit
Maximum payable per motor own-damage claim: ₹5,00,000 or the insured declared value, whichever is lower. Motor claims above ₹3,00,000 must be reviewed by a senior claims officer.

## MOT-3 Motor Required Documents
Required documents: FIR_COPY, REPAIR_ESTIMATE, DRIVING_LICENSE, POLICY_COPY. A police FIR copy is mandatory for accident claims involving injury, third parties or theft. A garage repair estimate is required to verify the claimed amount.

## MOT-4 Motor Exclusions
The following are excluded from motor cover: damage while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, driving without a valid driving licence, normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, racing or speed testing, and intentional damage.

## MOT-5 Motor Reporting Time
An accident must be reported to the insurer within 7 days of the incident. Late reporting requires a written explanation from the customer.

## MOT-6 Motor Deductible
A compulsory deductible of ₹2,000 applies to every own-damage claim and is subtracted from the settlement amount.

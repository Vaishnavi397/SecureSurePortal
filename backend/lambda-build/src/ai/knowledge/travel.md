## TRV-1 Travel Cover - Trip Cancellation
Non-refundable prepaid trip costs are covered when a trip is cancelled because of the insured person's sudden illness or hospitalisation, death of a close family member, natural disaster at the destination, or cancellation of the flight by the airline.

## TRV-2 Travel Claim Limit
Maximum payable per trip cancellation claim: ₹1,00,000. Lost checked baggage is covered up to ₹25,000 per trip.

## TRV-3 Travel Required Documents
Required documents: TICKET_COPY, BOOKING_INVOICE, CANCELLATION_PROOF, POLICY_COPY. CANCELLATION_PROOF means the airline or hotel cancellation confirmation, or a medical certificate for illness related cancellation.

## TRV-4 Travel Exclusions
The following are excluded from travel cover: cancellation due to change of mind or voluntary change of plans, travel against medical advice, pre-existing medical conditions, war or civil unrest known before booking, and cancellation caused by failure to obtain a visa on time.

## TRV-5 Travel Notification
The insurer must be notified of a trip cancellation as soon as possible and within 30 days of the cancellation date.
